About

A Library and Information Science professional with nearly two decades of experience in bibliographic control, metadata management, ILS implementation, research information management and team leadership, driven by a commitment to promoting literacy and lifelong learning.

He is currently a Professional Assistant at the Central Library, Pondicherry University. Previously, he was associated with IIT Madras, one of India’s premier institutions, and the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD), Kerala.

Research interests: scientometrics and bibliometrics, webometrics, research data repositories, library automation, research support and metadata.
